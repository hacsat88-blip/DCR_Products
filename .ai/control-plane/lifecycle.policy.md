# AI Control Plane Lifecycle Policy

## Classes

- `source-of-truth`: editable canonical content.
- `generated-target`: generated by deploy scripts; do not edit directly.
- `runtime-state`: local tool state; do not commit.
- `external-runtime`: owned by the vendor tool; inspect metadata only.
- `vendor-cache`: cache or marketplace checkout; do not promote without intake.
- `candidate-import`: useful external content that may be selectively imported after review.
- `forbidden-secret`: credential or token-adjacent path; never read content.

## Migration Stages

1. `mapped`: old and new locations are declared in registries.
2. `dual-read`: tools can resolve old and new locations.
3. `dual-write`: deploy or registry generation can refresh both locations.
4. `new-primary`: a mapped `.ai/assets` or `.ai/core` path becomes the first canonical content location.
5. `legacy-thin`: old paths become reference stubs or compatibility entrypoints.
6. `retired`: old paths are removed only after drift checks and user approval.

Retired runtime targets that must not be restored as active distribution
surfaces are declared in `retired-targets.policy.json` and enforced by
`tools/test-retired-targets.ps1`.
